# DOOM Card

Retro DOOM-like first-person shooter game as a Home Assistant Lovelace card!

## Features

- 🎮 Full 3D ray-casting engine
- 🔫 Multiple weapons (pistol, shotgun, chaingun)
- 👾 Enemies with AI behavior
- 🗺️ Multiple levels
- 🎵 Retro sound effects
- ⌨️ Keyboard and mouse controls
- 📊 Score tracking and statistics

## Controls

- **I** - Move forward
- **K** - Move backward
- **J** - Turn left
- **L** - Turn right
- **Mouse Movement** - Look around (auto-locks on start)
- **Left Click** or **Space** - Shoot
- **Right Click** - Cycle weapons
- **Mouse Wheel** - Switch weapons
- **1-3** - Quick weapon select
- **ESC** - Pause menu

Experience classic DOOM-style gameplay right in your Home Assistant dashboard!
